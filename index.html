About

Water, Code, & Uncertainty

I am a water resources engineer and researcher specializing in stochastic hydrology, meteorology, and engineering applications. My research improves modeling of short-duration extreme rainfall for urban infrastructure design, climate resilience, and flood risk management.

Advisor: Dr. Claudio Meier, University of Memphis

"Science, to me, is a process of decoding the unseen — especially where water and uncertainty meet..."
